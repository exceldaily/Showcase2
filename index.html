Our Story

A Family Table Open to Everyone

Ace's Restaurant is locally owned and operated by the Bright family. We have been serving great country style food since 2001.

From the moment you walk through our door, you're treated like family. We believe good food and good company go hand in hand — and that a hearty home-cooked meal can make any day better.

Whether you're stopping in for a full breakfast, a country plate at lunch, or our beloved Saturday Country Buffet, you'll always find a warm welcome and a full plate waiting for you.

The Saturday Country Buffet

Starting May 30th, join us every Saturday for our Country Buffet. Load up your plate with all the country fixin's you love, along with your choice of select homestyle meats.

It's the perfect way to spend a Saturday evening with family and friends — good food, good people, and plenty of it.
